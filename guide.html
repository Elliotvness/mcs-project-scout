Section 01 · Standard Operating Procedure

Step-by-Step Daily Team Workflow

Follow these 6 steps from initial signal discovery to consultative outreach and pipeline tracking
STEP 01 Access & Sync

Launch the Dashboard & Verify Live Feed

Open the cloud dashboard at mcs-project-scout.onrender.com (or elliotvness.github.io/mcs-project-scout) or run Start_Local_Server.bat on your laptop (http://localhost:8000).

  • Top KPI Ribbon: Instantly check total active projects (48), estimated pipeline volume, total pallet positions, and Tier-1 Hot count.
  • Offline Resilience: Even if opened directly from OneDrive (index.html) without internet, all 48 leads and contacts are embedded and load automatically.
  • Rescan Button: Click Rescan in the top-right header to reload the latest leads.json feed into memory.
STEP 02 Triage & Filter

Filter by Project Lifecycle, Market & Priority Grade

Use the Lifecycle Ribbon and filter dropdowns to isolate the exact type of opportunity you are hunting:

  • Tenant Leases / New Builds (NEW_CONSTRUCTION): Newly signed 100K–692K SF occupier leases (e.g., Titanium Plus Auto Parts in Chino, Lionext in Santa Fe Springs, DCW in Rancho Dominguez).
  • Building Permits (BUILDING_PERMIT): Active LADBS structural/warehouse alteration permits where tenants are modifying high-pile storage.
  • Relocations / Closures (FACILITY_RELOCATION): WARN notices requiring rack teardown, anchor flush-grinding/epoxy slab restoration, and new facility re-permitting.
  • Gov / Military RFPs (GOVERNMENT_RFP): Base armory, evidence, and public works high-density storage contracts.
STEP 03 GIS & Dock Recon

Inspect the Facility via Interactive GIS Map & Google Satellite

Every project is geocoded on the interactive Southern California & Western US Logistics Map on the left pane (and inside the slide-over drawer’s mini-map).

  • Color-Coded Pins: Red = Tier-1 Hot (Grade A+ / Urgent ≥4.15), Amber = Tier-2 Warm (Grade A/B).
  • Satellite Dock Inspection: Inside any lead drawer, toggle the mini-map to Satellite or click Inspect Docks (Google Satellite) to count dock-high doors, staging yard depth, and truck court clearance before calling the prospect.
  • Driving Route: Click Get Driving Route to Facility to generate turn-by-turn directions from McMurray Stern HQ (15511 Carmenita Rd, Santa Fe Springs).
STEP 04 Contacts, Dates & CRM

Review or Edit Contacts, Project Dates & LinkedIn Targets

Click any project card to open the Slide-Over Dossier Drawer and scroll to the Primary Decision-Maker & Timeline panel:

  • Pre-Populated Decision-Makers: All 48 projects include verified executive names (CEO, SVP Supply Chain, COO) and transaction brokers (DAUM, Lee & Associates, CBRE, JLL, Klabin, Voit) with direct phones and emails.
  • Live Contact & Date Editor: Update Contact Name, Title, Company/Broker, Email, Phone, Filing/Lease Date, or Target Move-In Date and click Save Contact & Dates. Changes persist to the server (leads.json) and browser storage.
  • One-Click LinkedIn Lookup: Click Search Target on LinkedIn to launch a pre-filtered LinkedIn People search for that exact company and executive/broker.
STEP 05 BOM & Sales Pitch

Run the Live Racking Estimator & Copy the Outreach Dossier

While on the phone with a broker or facility manager, use the interactive Turnkey Racking & BOM Calculator inside the drawer:

  • What-If Sizing: Type a new Square Footage or Clear Height (ft) and click Recalculate. The engine recalculates Pallet Capacity, Total Bays, Upright Frames, Step Beams, Flared Wire Decks, Seismic Wedge Anchors, and the Turnkey Budgetary Range ($82–$108/pallet position).
  • Ready-to-Send Email & Phone Script: Scroll to the Sales Outreach Battlecard to copy the tailored email subject, body (pitching a complimentary preliminary CAD layout + California Ch. 32 High-Pile permit packet), and cold-call opener script.
STEP 06 Outreach & Pipeline Tracking

Send 1-Click Outlook Outreach, Track Pipeline Stage & Export CSV

Execute outreach directly from the project card or drawer and track deal progression:

  • 1-Click Outlook Integration: Click Outlook Web or Outlook App to open a pre-addressed, consultative email with the facility’s preliminary pallet capacity, bay count, and turnkey permitting value proposition—automatically marking the lead CONTACTED.
  • Update Pipeline Stage: Progress the status dropdown from NEW → CONTACTED → SITE_WALK → PROPOSAL_SENT → WON and click Export CSV anytime for weekly sales pipeline reviews.

Section 03 · Qualification Methodology

5-Dimension Weighted Lead Scoring Matrix & Signal Rules

Weighted Formula (1–5 Scale per Dimension)

Total = (Recency×0.20) + (Size×0.15) + (NewSpace×0.20) + (Growth×0.20) + (Reachability×0.25)
Dimension Weight Score 5 Score 4 Score 3 Score 1–2
1. Recency 0.20 ≤ 1 month 1–3 months 3–6 months > 6 months
2. Size Fit 0.15 100K–500K SF 50K–100K or 500K–1M 25K–50K or 1M+ SF < 25K SF / Unknown
3. New Space 0.20 New lease / Empty shell Pre-lease / Build-to-Suit Facility expansion Renewal / No change
4. Growth 0.20 Hiring + New market DC Steady 3PL/Mfg growth Stable operations Consolidating / Distressed
5. Reachability 0.25 Named exec + direct phone/email Named contact via broker Company ID’d, no direct contact Confidential tenant (Score 2)
≥ 4.15 → URGENT Same-day broker & executive outreach + preliminary CAD offer
3.50 – 4.14 → HIGH Contact within 48 hours; track construction delivery schedule
< 3.50 → NORMAL Monitor for tenant naming or permit pull

Mandatory Signal & Compliance Rules

Pursue (Strong Signals)

New warehouse leases (50K+ SF), occupier building purchases, build-to-suit DCs, 3PL expansions, and LADBS high-pile racking permits.

Strictly Excluded
  • Amazon Facilities: Excluded (self-performs racking).
  • Cold Storage / Food Processing: Excluded (specialized vendor space).
  • Lease Renewals & Investor Flips: Excluded (racking already installed).
  • CoStar / LoopNet: Never scraped or referenced (strict TOS compliance).
High-Piled Storage Permits (PRA Protocol)

CFC Chapter 32 High-Piled Combustible Storage permits are obtained via Public Records Act (PRA) requests to local Fire Prevention Bureaus (Santa Fe Springs Fire, Chino Valley Fire, LA County Fire, OCFA).
